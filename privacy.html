Kitab Reader — Privacy Policy

Kitab Reader is a personal book reader. This page explains, in plain words, what the app does with your data.

Your books stay on your device

Books you read — from your phone, or downloaded from your Google Drive at your request — are stored only inside the app on your own device. They are never uploaded to us or to any server. We run no server that receives your books, reading history, or notes.

Google Drive access

  • If you connect Google Drive, the app asks Google for permission in one of two ways: read-only access to your Drive (so your books can arrive on their own), or access only to files you personally pick in Google's file picker.
  • The app uses that access only to list and download book files (PDF, EPUB, TXT) that you own, directly from Google to your device.
  • Your Google sign-in happens on Google's own pages. The app never sees your Google password.
  • You can disconnect Drive at any time from the app's menu, and you can remove the app's access completely from your Google Account's security settings.

No tracking, no ads

The app contains no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party trackers. Your annotations and reading progress are stored on your device only.
